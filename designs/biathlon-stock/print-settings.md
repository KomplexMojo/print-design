# biathlon-stock: print settings

Bambu Studio user presets for the Bambu H2S + AMS 2 Pro live in
`printer/presets/biathlon-stock/` (generated by `make_presets.py` there). Each one inherits
an H2S system preset and overrides only what this stock needs. Parent names were checked
against the Bambu Studio install on 2026-11-23.

## Import
1. Bambu Studio > File > Import > Import Configs. Select all 15 JSON files in
   `printer/presets/biathlon-stock/{machine,process,filament}/`.
2. Pick printer **H2S 0.4 hardened - Stock proto** (prototypes) or
   **H2S 0.6 hardened - Stock final** (final), then the matching filament and process below.
3. For `printloop slice`, run `printloop profiles` after importing; the `stock-*` variants in
   `printer/slicer.yaml` then resolve.

## Prototype set (speed, low cost, 0.4 mm hardened nozzle, 0.24 mm layers)

| Part | Process preset | Filament | Walls | Infill | Top / bottom | Supports | Brim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rear stock | Stock proto rear 0.24 @H2S 0.4 | Stock PLA Basic proto (indoors) or Stock PETG HF outdoor | 3 | 15 % grid | 4 / 4 | painted only | auto |
| Main body | Stock proto main 0.24 @H2S 0.4 | same | 4 | 20 % grid | 4 / 4 | painted only | 5 mm outer |
| Forestock | Stock proto fore 0.24 @H2S 0.4 | same | 3 | 15 % grid | 4 / 4 | painted only | auto |

- PLA 220 °C, textured PEI 55 °C, no chamber heat. PETG HF 245 °C, plate 70 °C, fan capped at 40 % for layer bond.
- Drying in the AMS 2 Pro: PLA 50 °C 8 h if the spool has been open over a week; PETG 65 °C 8 h.
- Prototype strength modifiers are optional; add the joint ones below at 6 walls / 60 % if bolts are torqued.

## Final set (strength and cold toughness, 0.6 mm hardened nozzle, 0.24 mm layers)

| Part | Process preset | Filament | Walls | Infill | Top / bottom | Supports | Brim |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rear stock | Stock final rear 0.24 @H2S 0.6 | Stock PAHT-CF final | 6 (3.7 mm) | 35 % gyroid | 7 / 6 | painted only | 8 mm outer |
| Main body | Stock final main 0.24 @H2S 0.6 | Stock PAHT-CF final | 8 (5 mm) | 50 % gyroid | 7 / 6 | painted only | 10 mm outer |
| Forestock | Stock final fore 0.24 @H2S 0.6 | Stock PAHT-CF final | 6 | 40 % gyroid | 7 / 6 | painted only | 8 mm outer |
| Accessories | Stock final ASA accessory 0.24 @H2S 0.6 | Stock ASA final | 4 | 40 % gyroid | 5 / 5 | tree auto | 5 mm outer |
| Butt pad | Stock final TPU pad 0.24 @H2S 0.6 | Stock TPU 90A pad | 3 | 20 % gyroid (cushion) | 5 / 4 | none | none |

- **PAHT-CF:** 290 °C, chamber 65 °C (H2S max), Engineering or textured plate 100 °C with glue,
  part fan 0–20 % (more fan weakens layers), max 8 mm³/s, so walls run at 60 mm/s.
  Dry 80 °C for 12 h in a separate dryer and feed from it through the **external spool**
  (AMS 2 Pro tops out at 65 °C and does not take PAHT-CF). Let the chamber soak 15 min before starting.
- **ASA:** 270 °C, chamber 65 °C, plate 100 °C, fan ≤ 25 %. AMS 2 Pro, dry 65 °C 12 h.
- **TPU 90A:** 225 °C, plate 35 °C, external spool only. The pad prints flat on its mounting
  face, not on its side: it is a cushion, not a load path. (TPU for AMS is 68D, too hard for a pad.)
- Infill and walls add mass, which helps reach the IBU 3.5 kg rifle minimum; weigh before adding ballast.

## Supports
All stock processes use **tree (manual)**: nothing gets support unless painted. Paint
enforcers (support painting tool) only on:
- action inlet ceiling and the barrel channel roof (bedding surfaces, keep them true),
- mag-well ceiling and trigger slot,
- joint sockets (rear tongue socket, front dovetail socket) and cross-dowel bores if not teardropped.

Interfaces: 3 top layers, 0.24 mm gap (0.2 mm on PLA). Do not paint the IBU marking surfaces;
they are side faces and need no support.

## Strength modifiers (add per part in Bambu Studio)
Right-click the part > Add modifier > Box or Cylinder, place it, then set on the modifier:
**wall loops 10, sparse infill 100 %, pattern rectilinear**. Positions from r1 `PARAMS`
(X from the trigger blade, forward positive):

| Zone | Part | Modifier |
|---|---|---|
| Rear joint, tongue and 2 × M6 bolts | rear + main | box X −148 to −68, full section |
| Wrist (grip to rear joint) | main | box X −68 to −30, **60 % gyroid**, 10 walls |
| Action pillars / screws | main | cylinder Ø 30 mm around each screw, full depth |
| Front joint, dovetail, tie rods, cross-dowels | main + fore | box X 195 to 265, full section |
| Harness rail inserts | fore | cylinder Ø 25 mm at X 270 and 350 |
| Hand stop and sling slot | fore | box around the hand stop (X ~370 to 435) |
| Butt hook root, butt-pad inserts | rear | box over the hook root; Ø 20 mm at each pad insert |

Because the parts lie on their side, the bolt and insert axes are vertical in the print:
the wall loops wrap every hole, so 8–10 walls already give ~5–6 mm of solid ring.

## Threaded inserts (heat-set brass)
Model holes with `printloop.fits` hole shrink in mind; these are the insert maker's nominal
sizes (Ruthex / CNC Kitchen style). Depth = insert length + 1.5 mm. Keep ≥ 2 mm plastic
outside the hole and ≥ 3 wall loops around it.

| Thread | Hole Ø | Insert length | Min hole depth | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M3 | 4.0 | 5.7 | 7.2 | covers, small accessories |
| M4 | 5.6 | 8.1 | 9.6 | butt-pad screws |
| M5 | 6.4 | 9.5 | 11.0 | harness rail (r1 `insert_hole_d`) |
| M6 | 8.0 | 12.7 | 14.2 | accessory mounts only; joints use cross-dowels |

Iron temperature: PLA 210 °C, PETG 235 °C, ASA 245 °C, **PAHT-CF 280 °C** (press slowly, let
the melt flow, hold flat for 5 s). In PAHT-CF set inserts **after annealing**.

## PAHT-CF annealing and post-processing
1. Leave the part on the plate in the chamber until it cools below 40 °C (reduces warp).
2. Remove brim and support; deburr the inlet and sockets.
3. **Anneal** in an oven at 100 °C for 8 h, part lying on its side on a flat tray (or packed in
   fine sand for the long main body), ramp up and cool down in the oven over ~2 h each way.
   Bambu's window is 80–130 °C for 6–12 h. Print a 100 mm test bar with each batch and measure
   it before and after to get the shrink (expect ~0.1–0.3 % along layers).
4. Ream bores to size after annealing: cross-dowel bores (press, 0.10 mm), pillar bores, and
   any horizontal teardrop holes.
5. Set heat-set inserts, then bond cross-dowels and aluminium pillars (structural epoxy).
6. Skim-bed the action on the pillars (release agent on the action).
7. Seal: thin coat of matte clear or paint on all faces, plus flexible sealant or an O-ring in
   each joint line, so meltwater cannot reach the infill. PAHT-CF picks up moisture, which
   changes size slightly; sealing keeps the zero stable from −30 to +30 °C.
8. Before the race build: freeze a coupon to −30 °C and do a drop test (brief open question 7).

## IBU flags
None of these settings change geometry, so they cannot break the IBU limits. Two to watch:
- Weight: the final infill gives a heavy stock. That helps the 3.5 kg minimum; it never hurts compliance.
- Marking surfaces must stay flat: do not use fuzzy skin or ironing patterns there, and keep supports off them.
